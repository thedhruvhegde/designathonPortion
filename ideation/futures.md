# Futures: 12 concepts, simulated

Each concept is run forward through one plausible future: **Hour 0 → Day 1 → Week 1 → Month 1 → Year 1**, plus a **failure future** (how it most likely dies). The impermanence mechanic is marked: `disappear` · `expire` · `transform` · `decay`.

---

## A. Half-Life — notes that forget the way you do · `decay`
Study notes fade on an Ebbinghaus forgetting curve. Reviewing a note brings it back. Notes you never return to dissolve, and an LLM squeezes each one into a one-line "essence" that stays as its fossil.
- **Hour 0:** Student pastes lecture notes. Each paragraph gets a half-life.
- **Day 1:** About 30% of the text is visibly faded. The fading shows them what they never looked at again.
- **Week 1:** Before the midterm, the faded parts become the study list. Reviewing makes text sharp again.
- **Month 1:** The notebook is 60% smaller, and what is left is what mattered.
- **Year 1:** A semester comes down to one page of essences. Exams become "rebuild what faded."
- **Failure future:** Students panic about losing notes, ask for an "archive" button, and the idea turns into Anki with extra steps.

## B. Mayfly — group chats that are born to die · `expire`
Every group (trip, class project, party) gets a lifespan when it is created. At death it leaves a "fossil": 10 highlight photos, the decisions, and who owes whom.
- **Hour 0:** "Spring Break Tulum" chat is created with a 14-day life.
- **Week 1:** People post more freely because they know it ends.
- **Month 1:** The chat has dissolved. The fossil card lives in each person's profile.
- **Year 1:** The average user has 40% fewer dead group chats.
- **Failure future:** It's a feature, not a product. WhatsApp ships "group expiry" in a single update.

## C. Rain Check — campus notes that weather away · `decay`
Leave a location-pinned note (AR or map) anywhere on campus. It decays with real weather: rain washes it out, sun bleaches it, heavy foot traffic smudges it. No delete button. The world deletes it.
- **Hour 0:** "Free pizza, Bob & Betty Beyster lobby" is chalked onto a map pin.
- **Day 1:** It rains 4 mm, the note is half-washed, and a live Open-Meteo feed drives the shader.
- **Week 1:** The campus map turns into a living, self-cleaning bulletin board, with no stale flyers.
- **Month 1:** Clubs replace paper flyers (paper waste down, and no moderation backlog of old posts).
- **Year 1:** City version: neighborhood chalk layer, sunny cities hold memories longer.
- **Failure future:** Moderation and harassment at named locations. Needs report + fast-decay-on-flag.

## D. Melt — the move-out market where prices melt · `decay → transform`
At move-out, students list furniture, mini-fridges and lamps. Every listing's price melts toward $0 on a public curve. At $0 it turns into a free claim, and if nobody claims it, it becomes a scheduled donation pickup. Nothing ends in the dumpster.
- **Hour 0:** Futon listed at $80 with a 72-hour melt.
- **Day 1:** $52. Buyers watch it like a stock. Timing it becomes a game.
- **Week 1 (move-out week):** Thousands of listings melt. Dorm dumpster tonnage drops.
- **Month 1 (move-in):** The unclaimed pool goes back to incoming freshmen.
- **Year 1:** University sustainability office licenses it, and the impact dashboard (lbs diverted) becomes the pitch.
- **Failure future:** Low liquidity at small schools. Fix by seeding with the RA/res-life inventory.

## E. Dissolve — worries you write and watch disappear · `disappear`
A journaling ritual: type the worry, then physically "drop" it into water, fire, or wind. The ink dissolves in a fluid simulation. Nothing is stored. You get only an anonymous count ("14,203 worries let go today"). Backed by Briñol et al. (2013, *Psychological Science*), where writing thoughts on paper and throwing it away reduced how much people used them, and by Kanaya & Kawai (2024, *Scientific Reports*, Nagoya), where anger returned to baseline only when the written note was thrown away or shredded.
- **Hour 0:** "I'm going to fail orgo" is dropped into water and dissolves in 8 s.
- **Day 1:** A 30-second nightly ritual. The value is that it's gone.
- **Week 1:** An optional mood check-in before and after shows self-reported relief.
- **Month 1:** Campus counseling centers use it as an on-ramp ("still heavy? talk to someone").
- **Year 1:** Physical twin: dissolving-paper booths at finals week.
- **Failure future:** Seen as a toy. Needs the research framing and a real crisis-escalation path.

## F. Ghostfile — a camera roll that composts · `decay`
Screenshots, duplicate photos and memes you never open slowly grain out and fade. At full fade they "compost" into storage freed and grams of CO₂ saved. Tap to keep anything you want.
- **Day 1:** 2,300 screenshots flagged. A grain shader shows what is dying.
- **Month 1:** 11 GB reclaimed. The camera roll is reduced to the photos that matter.
- **Failure future:** Fear of loss. Platform photo-library permissions are hard to use in a web demo.

## G. Bloom Pass — tickets that become plants · `transform`
Event wristbands made of seed paper, with a digital twin that grows as you attend.
- **Failure future:** Physical production is hard in a hackathon, and seed paper is a known gimmick.

## H. Half-Life Consent — permissions with an expiry date · `expire`
App access to your location, contacts and camera decays by default unless you re-consent.
- **Failure future:** Abstract. Hard to make judges *feel* it in 3 minutes.

## I. Sunset Letters — messages you can only read at golden hour · `expire`
A letter unlocks only during the sender's local sunset and fades when the sun goes down.
- **Failure future:** Delightful, but the problem it solves is thin.

## J. Spore Commons — mycelium pop-up furniture · `decay`
Temporary campus seating grown from mycelium that composts after the semester.
- **Failure future:** It can't be grown or demoed in a weekend.

## K. Ghost Pack — dissolving dining-hall packaging · `disappear`
Seaweed-film sauce and condiment packs for dining halls.
- **Failure future:** Notpla already exists. Low originality, nothing to demo.

## L. Leftover — dining surplus alerts that vanish · `expire`
Dining-hall surplus is posted with a live countdown that matches the food's perishability.
- **Failure future:** Too close to existing food-rescue apps (Too Good To Go, Olio).
